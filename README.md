# DIGITAL-PLANNER-ORGANIZER
Productivity & Budget Hub is an all-in-one planner for managing your money and your time in one place. Track your income and expenses, grow your savings, stay on top of loan payments, and plan upcoming bills with forecasts that always match what you owe. 

Keep your tasks organized with due dates, urgency levels and status tracking, then see everything together on a color-coded monthly calendar. Your data stays on your own device, with easy backup and restore whenever you need it.

Budget
Record your income and expenses and see where your money goes. Filter by month and year, and view charts comparing income, expenses and forecasted amounts, plus your remaining loans and savings balances.

Savings
Track your bank savings accounts in one place. Add accounts with an initial balance and interest rate, record deposits and withdrawals, and see your running balance, total savings and full transaction history.

Loans
Keep track of money you owe or money owed to you. Add a loan with its amount, installment and due dates, then record a payment of any amount at any time. Balances update automatically, every payment is logged in the payment history, and the loan shows as paid off once it reaches zero.

Forecast
Plan upcoming income and expenses before they happen. Loan installments are scheduled automatically and always add up to your remaining loan balance. Entries are grouped by 15th and 30th cutoffs, and you can edit amounts and dates or mark items as paid or canceled.

Tasks
Manage your to-do list with status, urgency, category, schedule, links and remarks. View tasks coming up within 7 days or see all tasks. Search and filter them, check today’s progress on the status graph, and export your list to CSV.

Calendar
See your tasks, transactions and forecasts together on one monthly calendar. Color-coded entries make it easy to spot work, personal items, income, expenses, loan payments and forecasts at a glance.

Settings
Personalize your hub by changing its title and adding custom budget categories. Download a full backup of your data, restore it on any device, or clear everything from the current device.
